Smart parking, run from one screen.

ParkEase shows which slots are free in real time, records vehicles in and out by number plate, bills automatically, and lets drivers book a slot in advance. Built with MongoDB, Express, React and Node.js.

See the screens View the roadmap

The problem

Most small and mid-sized parking lots still track cars on paper. Staff don't know what is free, cars get lost, two people can assign the same slot, and billing at exit depends on memory.

Unknown availability

No one knows how many slots are free until they drive in.

Double assignment

Two staff members can park two cars in the same slot.

Manual billing

Fees are calculated by hand, which leads to errors and disputes.

What it does

Built in phases. Each phase works on its own and is tagged in Git.

Phase 1 · Manager panel

Facilities and layouts

  • Drag-and-drop layout designer (slots, roads, gates, arrows)
  • Draft and publish, bulk slot naming, copy from another floor
  • Add floors from the designer
Phase 1 · Operations

Entry and exit

  • Number plate read from a photo (Tesseract.js), verified by staff
  • Slot claimed atomically: no double parking
  • Fee calculated on the server, receipt printed
Phase 1 · Dashboard

Live view

  • Floor-wise availability and live floor plan
  • Updates through Socket.IO, no refresh needed
  • Slot status filters, grid and map views
Phase 1 · History and money

History and payments

  • Filter by vehicle, date, status
  • Sort by time, amount, duration, or payment type
  • CSV export, payment summary
Phase 2 · Drivers

Driver portal and bookings

  • Driver sign-up with consent, saved vehicles
  • Book a time window; double-booking blocked in the database
  • Cancel up to 1 hour before start
  • Live public availability page (counts only)
Phase 3 · Payments (demo)

Booking payments

  • Paid bookings held for 15 minutes until paid
  • Demo payment with receipt and refund record
  • Razorpay replaces the demo in Phase 3

Roadmap

What is done, what is next, and what comes after.

Done

Phase 1: Manager panel

Facilities, layout designer, slots, dashboard, entry and exit, fees, history and payments.

Done

Phase 2: Driver portal, bookings, public page

Driver sign-up, vehicles, reservations, double-booking protection, live public availability, admin driver monitoring.

In progress

Phase 3: Payments

Demo payment is built. Next: Razorpay SDK, checkout, signature verification, webhook, real refunds, and an online option at exit.

Planned

Phase 4: Reports and analytics

Daily, weekly and monthly occupancy trends. Revenue split by cash, UPI, card and online. Peak-hour charts and slot-wise use. PDF and Excel export. Filters by facility, floor and date range (Chart.js or Recharts).

Planned

Phase 5: Testing, hardening, deployment

Automated tests for fees, double-booking and auth. Frontend flow tests. Full Content Security Policy and subresource integrity. Cookie-based login. Logs and error monitoring with retention. Accessibility audit. Keep-alive or paid hosting. API documentation.
